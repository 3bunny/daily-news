Read more
Blackmagic Design has introduced an Ambisonic Microphone, a compact, faceted metal sphere housing 108 microphones. This device is designed to capture 360-degree immersive audio, providing a matching soundtrack for 360-degree cameras and virtual reality (VR) content. The significance lies in addressing a common limitation of immersive video: while visuals can be all-encompassing, audio often remains static or less immersive. By integrating a high-density array of MEMS (Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems) microphones, the device can record sound from every direction. This allows content creators to produce Ambisonic audio, a full-sphere surround sound format that accurately reproduces the acoustic environment. When paired with VR headsets or 360-degree video players, this technology enables listeners to experience sound that shifts realistically as they turn their heads, enhancing the sense of presence and immersion in the virtual or recorded environment, making the overall experience far more compelling and realistic.
