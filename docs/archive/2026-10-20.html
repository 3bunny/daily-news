This upgrade is crucial because efficient munitions management is fundamental to military readiness and operational success. A modern, robust system like TACTMIS ensures that the correct types and quantities of ammunition are available precisely when and where they are needed, minimizing logistical delays, reducing waste, and directly supporting combat operations. It addresses the limitations of older systems, integrating contemporary technologies and data management practices for improved accuracy and responsiveness. In context, munitions logistics are inherently complex, involving the tracking of vast inventories, managing diverse ammunition types across various locations, ensuring safety protocols, and coordinating supply chains from manufacturing to the frontline. An outdated system can lead to critical shortages, overstocking, or significant delays, directly impacting military effectiveness. TACTMIS aims to provide better visibility and control over the Army's ammunition assets, which is vital for large-scale operations and maintaining a strategic advantage.

arXiv:2610.23284v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Every study of why LLM agents fail begins by labelling traces by hand, and every such study builds its own throwaway tool. Observability platforms record agent traces but offer no structured labelling; annotation tools label text but have no notion of a step, a delegation or a tool call, leaving it unclear what the smallest unit of execution to label should be. Annotrace reads six trace formats, including OpenTelemetry GenAI, LangSmith and Langfuse, and turns each run into an ordered, nested list of steps. The annotator writes their own labelling codebook; a frontier model proposes a starting set from a sample of their own traces, answers first, and is asked exactly the questions the human is asked, making annotation a joint human-AI process. Annotrace measures agreement between any two annotators, human or AI. On a multi-agent question-answering corpus it reproduces a previous manual effort's 235 annotation units exactly, and two models labelling the same traces agree 91% of the time at $\kappa = 0.82$.
