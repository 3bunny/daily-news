This announcement, made at Euronaval 2026, means the frigates will carry twice as many interceptors. This enhancement is crucial for several reasons. Doubling the VLS cells dramatically increases the frigates' air defense capabilities, allowing them to counter more numerous or complex aerial threats, such as advanced anti-ship missiles, aircraft, or drone swarms, over a sustained period. The Aster 30 missile, which these frigates will primarily use, provides area air defense, meaning the ships can protect not only themselves but also other valuable assets within a naval task group. This upgrade signals France's recognition of an evolving and more challenging threat landscape in modern naval warfare, requiring more robust and resilient defensive systems for its frontline warships. The FDI frigates are a new class of multi-role vessels intended to be the backbone of the French Navy's surface fleet, replacing older ships. Their original design already incorporated advanced stealth features and combat systems. The decision to enhance their missile capacity before full deployment underscores a strategic imperative to future-proof these critical assets against increasingly sophisticated adversaries and ensure their effectiveness in high-intensity conflicts. Read more
Operational F-15EX Eagle II fighter jets have begun flying with Conformal Fuel Tanks (CFTs), a significant development given the U.S. Air Force's initial plans for the aircraft. The F-15EX was primarily conceived as an air-to-air combat platform, leveraging its speed and payload capacity to complement stealth fighters. The adoption of CFTs indicates an evolution in its mission demands. Conformal Fuel Tanks are semi-permanently attached fuel tanks that conform to the aircraft's fuselage, offering a substantial increase in fuel capacity without occupying weapon pylons. This is crucial because it allows the F-15EX to maintain its full weapon carriage capability while significantly extending its operational range and endurance. This extended reach is vital for potential operations across vast distances, particularly in the Indo-Pacific theater, where aerial refueling assets might be contested or scarce. Longer endurance also means the aircraft can loiter over targets or patrol areas for extended periods. While CFTs add some weight and drag, potentially impacting extreme performance metrics, the trade-off is deemed acceptable for missions prioritizing range, payload, and sustained presence. This move enhances the F-15EX's versatility, transforming it into an even more adaptable multi-role platform capable of undertaking a wider array of missions, including long-range strike or reconnaissance, in addition to its primary air superiority role. It underscores the Air Force's strategic shift towards greater operational flexibility and reach in a complex global security environment.
