This innovation addresses a long-standing challenge in tunnel construction: traditional TBMs are highly efficient in stable ground but struggle significantly when encountering extremely hard rock or mixed geological conditions, often requiring a switch to slower, more disruptive conventional blasting methods or even disassembly. The BBM aims to overcome this by seamlessly integrating both techniques, allowing it to adapt to varying ground conditions without major operational interruptions. This development could substantially accelerate the construction of critical infrastructure projects like railways, subways, and water tunnels, making them faster, safer, and more cost-effective by reducing downtime and improving efficiency in challenging environments.
