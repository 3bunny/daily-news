The "uphill path" refers to the significant complexities involved in integrating "quantum-resistant" cryptography (PQC) into Bitcoin's decentralized network. The effort to adopt PQC is crucial for Bitcoin's future resilience, necessitating broad consensus and meticulous development to safeguard the network against a future quantum threat without compromising its present integrity.
