A German intelligence report warns that Russia may launch a satellite carrying a nuclear weapon into orbit by the end of the year. If confirmed, this would constitute a grave violation of the 1967 Outer Space Treaty, which explicitly prohibits placing nuclear weapons or any other weapons of mass destruction in Earth's orbit. Such a deployment would dramatically escalate global tensions, potentially triggering a new arms race in space and destabilizing international security. A nuclear detonation in space, even if not directly targeting another satellite, could generate an electromagnetic pulse (EMP) or create a vast debris field, threatening critical space infrastructure essential for communication, navigation, and intelligence gathering worldwide. This development would represent a dangerous new frontier in strategic competition and a profound challenge to established international norms.
