Policymakers, technologists, and members of the public have raised concerns about the harm AI’s rapid advancement might cause to society. Their concerns include bias, data privacy violations, the spread of misinformation, and losing control of AI agents. Those issues are relatively new to many people, but the IEEE Standards Association has been working for the past decade on ways to prioritize the responsible development of AI systems. In 2016 IEEE SA launched the IEEE Global Initiative for Ethical Considerations in Autonomous and Intelligent Systems (A/IS) and published the Ethically Aligned Design report. EAD ’s mission is to encourage everyone involved in designing autonomous and intelligent systems to prioritize human well-being by applying ethical considerations for the benefit of humanity. Developed by a multidisciplinary community of experts from around the world, the report outlined eight foundational principles for A/IS. They include human rights, people’s well-being, and transparency. More than 100 leading AI ethics experts provided input. EAD ’s Version II report was released in 2017.
